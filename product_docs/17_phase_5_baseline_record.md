# Phase 5 baseline and G3 measurement record

**Date:** 8 October 2026  
**Decision:** D-49  
**Scope:** full local MOT17-02/04/05 replay on the Jetson Orin Nano, detector-every-frame mode. Phone capture is excluded.

The frozen run used YOLOX-Tiny TensorRT FP32, confidence `0.10`, NMS IoU `0.45`, MOT matching IoU `0.50`, visibility minimum `0.20`, a 30 FPS deadline of `33.333333 ms`, five warm-up frames excluded from steady-state latency, and two repetitions in the order MOT17-02-FRCNN, MOT17-04-FRCNN, MOT17-05-FRCNN. Every sequence completed twice with zero source-input drops.

The official TrackEval combined result over the three sequences is HOTA(0) `0.4554`, DetA(0) `0.4353`, AssA(0) `0.4764`, IDF1 `0.3970`, MOTA `0.2332`, 698 ID switches, and 1,213 fragmentations. The dependency-free metrics remain in the per-run reports as a reproducible cross-check; the TrackEval result is the authoritative Phase 5 metric record.

The complete-pipeline sustained run lasted 60.72 seconds and processed 1,238 frames. `tegrastats` recorded 60 samples: RAM 3,233–3,254 MB of 7,485 MB, junction temperature 44.593–45.5°C, GR3D utilization 0–99%, and VDD_IN 4,864–6,259 mW as onboard diagnostics. The board was in 25 W mode with the stock active heatsink/fan on the open benchtop at the recorded 27°C ambient. The telemetry format did not expose throttling flags. The earlier 30-minute detector-only thermal report remains separate and is not presented as complete-pipeline evidence.

The frozen limits are [`g3_limits.json`](../race_mot/configs/g3_limits.json): 55 ms p95 latency, 55% maximum DMR, 4,096 MB RAM, 55°C junction temperature, two repetitions, and 0.02 absolute HOTA/IDF1 non-inferiority margins for later policy comparisons. A 10% useful-energy reduction target is frozen for policy evaluation, but whole-device energy is currently unavailable because no external Jetson-input meter was connected. G3 is therefore measured but not fully closed; adaptive-policy implementation remains blocked only by the external-meter energy requirement and any final supervisor acceptance of the recorded limits.
